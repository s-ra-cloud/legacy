---
name: Quotation editorial policy
description: Why landing-page quotations must remain source-reviewed rather than taking CMS wording on trust.
---
Only display philosopher quotations that can be traced to a named work and a specific English translation, with a source link. Classical sayings preserved in a later text should identify that text.

**Why:** The user requested genuine landing-page quotations. The initial CMS and fallback copy included widespread misattributions and modern paraphrases, including Will Durant's summary presented as Aristotle's own words. CMS content is not evidence of authenticity.

**How to apply:** When adding a philosopher or changing quote copy, verify attribution and wording against the cited edition before exposing it publicly. Do not restore unreviewed CMS quotations just to make content management easier.
