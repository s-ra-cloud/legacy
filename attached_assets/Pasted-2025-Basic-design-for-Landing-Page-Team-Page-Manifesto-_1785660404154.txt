2025


Basic design for Landing Page, Team Page, Manifesto : Creation of the project’s public identity through a streamlined landing page, a clear presentation of contributors, and a concise manifesto outlining mission, principles, long-term vision, and the values structuring open, AI-assisted scholarship.


Basic Library Interface (module 1 alpha) : Development of a basic user-friendly interface enabling exploration of classical texts with advanced tools for search, comparison, and annotation, ensuring seamless integration with UM6P servers 


Basic Knowledge Tree Interface (module 2 alpha) : Conception of a visual system that represents academic genealogies, enabling navigation through mentors, students, and intellectual lineages, and preparing the platform for public contributions and open-source publication.


BasicThe Great Conversation interface (module 3 alpha) : Creation of an basic interface that maps explicit and implicit connections between classical texts, offering chronological and bidimensional network visualizations that support exploratory research on intellectual traditions.


Expansion of institutional partnerships : Structured outreach to universities, archives, and laboratories to broaden participation, secure new datasets.


LGC Phase 1 : Wittgenstein, in partnership with the Wittgenstein Archives in Bergen, Norway


LGC Phase 2 : Hegel, in partnership with the 13/13 Seminar (Columbia)


Migration ouf of test environment


Integration of Knowledge Tree.io data with Academic Tree archives : Fusion of two major datasets into a unified, validated structure, harmonizing identifiers, metadata, and lineage links, in collaboration with the University of Chicago Knowledge Lab.


Automated Transcription of Bentham French Manuscripts : Implementation of an AI-driven OCR and transcription pipeline tailored to eighteenth- and nineteenth-century French handwriting, enabling rapid digitisation, searchable text, and scholarly annotation of Bentham manuscripts.


50 Mythic Texts from the Mediterranean Tradition : Curated acquisition, preparation, and publication of fifty foundational Mediterranean mythological texts, providing clean digital editions, metadata, and cross-linking capabilities for integration into the AI Library and The Great Conversation.


CRISP Integration : Integrate CRISP so that users can chat with webmasters.


Visual overhaul : Redesign of all user-facing interfaces for clarity, speed, and coherence across platforms, improving typography, navigation flow, colour hierarchy, and layout consistency to meet modern UX standards and support multilingual displays.


Public launch : Final deployment of the platform to the global academic community, supported by onboarding materials, a 6 month outreach campaign, and institutional announcements, marking the transition from testing to sustained, long-term production use.
